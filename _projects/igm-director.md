As IGM's director I led the program to top-five rankings in US News, the Princeton Review, and Animation Career Review; founded RIT Games, the university-wide portfolio of game and games-related programs; helped found RIT esports; and grew IGM faculty funding to roughly $8 million, creating the GeoGames Lab and the MAGIC building. I also built the administrative backend that RIT's new chairs and directors still use.
