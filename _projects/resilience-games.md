Resilience games get the people who run critical infrastructure, and the people who depend on it, to the same table. **Jack Voltaic 4.0** and its successor **Sector Down** are tabletop and digital games built with RIT's ESL Global Cybersecurity Institute and the Army Cyber Institute; Sector Down was a finalist in the I/ITSEC Serious Games Challenge (Best Government Audience Game, 2025).

Current work includes a componentized critical infrastructure resilience game engine, LLM-driven scenario generation (LLM-ResiGame), and demonstrations at Connections, the Natural Hazards Workshop, and the Disaster Gaming Conference.
