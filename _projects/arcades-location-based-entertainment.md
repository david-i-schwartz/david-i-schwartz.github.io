Games don't have to live on a screen with a standard gamepad. This topic collects my teaching on arcade machines, custom controllers, and play in physical spaces.

## Alternative controllers (2009)

In my Alternative Controllers seminar at RIT, the final project asked each team to design a game around a controller that did not exist yet, and to build that controller if they could.

The photos below show one team's build, *Oh No! Banjo*: a banjo rhythm game made in six weeks by a team of five. The controller combines the electronics of a wired Rock Band guitar with a tambourine body, MDF board, and six arcade buttons for strumming. The game was written in C# and XNA, with note charts authored as MIDI. The controller held up to heavy play at GDC.
