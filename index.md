David I. Schwartz, Ph.D., has worked in game design and development academia since 1999, when he pitched the idea that led to co-founding the Game Design Initiative at Cornell University. Building on that success, he joined the Rochester Institute of Technology in 2007 as a faculty member in game design and development. As Director of the School of Interactive Games and Media from 2015 to 2026, he co-founded RIT Games and guided RIT to world-leading growth in academic rankings and in the field.

His current research applies digital engineering and gamification across cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, and resilience games, while also forging new ground in chaplaincy, nursing, and wargames. He co-directs RIT's Collaboratory for Resiliency and Recovery and coordinates RIT Digital Twins.

Away from campus he plays drums in a metal band, builds things out of fuse beads, and likes a long hike. Those live under [Making & Interests](/interests/).
