{%- comment -%} Reads the "Add new" lists (_data/cv_new/*.yml, filled in through the "Add ·" forms in Pages CMS) and hands them to the templates, so a new item shows on the site the moment it is saved, without touching the numbered master lists in _data/cv/. Sets: new_pubs, new_press the templates split them by type or kind and print them above the numbered entries all_service new service rows, then the master rows all_grants new grants, then the master grants all_theses new students, then the master students New items always come first, in reverse file order (the last item in the Add file prints first), then the master rows in their own order. They are not sorted by year on purpose: a year that Pages CMS saves as a number in one item and as text in another would make Jekyll's sort fail and stop the whole build. A missing file, an empty file, or [] all just mean "nothing new". Use: {% include cv-new.html %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cv_none = "" | split: "," -%} {%- assign cvn = site.data.cv_new -%} {%- assign new_pubs = cvn.publications | default: cv_none | compact | reverse -%} {%- assign new_press = cvn.press | default: cv_none | compact | reverse -%} {%- assign new_service = cvn.service | default: cv_none | compact | reverse -%} {%- assign new_grants = cvn.grants | default: cv_none | compact | reverse -%} {%- assign new_theses = cvn.theses | default: cv_none | compact | reverse -%} {%- assign all_service = new_service | concat: site.data.cv.service -%} {%- assign all_grants = new_grants | concat: site.data.cv.funding.grants -%} {%- assign all_theses = new_theses | concat: site.data.cv.theses -%}
